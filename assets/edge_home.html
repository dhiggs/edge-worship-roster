FIRST TIME AT EDGE CHURCH

Edge Church is a vibrant, life-giving church where people from all walks of life can find community, grow in their relationship with Jesus and discover their place in God’s story. We’re one church in multiple locations, with one heart and one mission: Love God. Love People. Change the World.

Whether you’re exploring faith, returning to church, or simply looking for a place to belong, you’re welcome at Edge.

Our Guest Services team will be there to welcome you and help you find your way. Edge Kids is available during Sunday morning services for children from 10 months to Grade 7. We recommend arriving 15–20 minutes early if you need to check your child in. Parking is available in the surrounding streets, with limited parking on the property and dedicated bays for elderly and disabled guests.

And yes, tea and coffee are on us! Enjoy free tea and coffee, with treats, cold drinks and barista-made coffee also available to purchase at Sacred Brew Coffee Shop. We can’t wait to meet you!
